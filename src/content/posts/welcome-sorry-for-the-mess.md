To be honest, I've been itching to start a blog since I was in my 20s.

In the heyday of LiveJournal and Xanga, I was posting cryptic song lyrics and emo poems daily. There were no algorithms back then. No "growing your online audience."

But times have changed.

![dial up internet](/images/dial-up.gif)

I wrote the first version of this post on Substack in early 2025. Since then I've built a place of my own at wilmartin.com, so consider this the updated welcome. Same sentiment, fewer growth hacks.

My biggest hurdle to starting a blog was the ego awareness that comes with shining a spotlight on myself and asking other people to look at me. 😳 It feels icky, right?

Social media changed everything. I'm not trying to be overdramatic, but it's true. It introduced us to algorithms, curated and targeted content, and influencers.

Instead of processing our thoughts in long form writing, we now blast out half baked quips to get a reaction. The ones that get the most reaction get boosted to the top of the timeline to keep us scrolling for more.

Like everything else in history, the upside of technology has been diluted and weaponized for the sake of profit.

Wow. I sound like a grumpy old man, but I promise I'm not.

![old man yells at clouds](/images/old-man-yells-at-clouds.jpg)

*Not me, I swear.*

## So what is this place?

It's a mix, on purpose. Short posts for quick thoughts, the stuff I'd normally toss into a social feed, except I own it and nothing decides who gets to see it. Long posts like this one for when something needs more room. And reviews, mostly TV and movies, including a slow march through John Carpenter's filmography.

Yes, I'm aware that a microblog sounds like exactly what I just complained about. The difference is that nobody is ranking it.

Think of the long posts as the front door. The short ones are the living room.

## Who am I? What am I doing here?

The natural first question. I'll keep it brief because much of who I am will come out through my writing, but here's the TL;DR.

I'm a dude from small town South Carolina who was born in the 80s, grew up in the 90s, and didn't have a cell phone until college. I have a beautiful wife who loves gardening and 2 boys (a teenager and an elementary schooler) who are both incredible little guys. We have a dog, a home, a giant garden, and a lot of love.

I'm a graphic designer and an SEO specialist who works in marketing. I'm a business owner with a day job. So yeah, pretty busy.

My favorite TV show is Severance and my favorite movie is Mission Impossible 2.

That's good, for now I think.

## Why do this at all?

A few reasons.

**We could all use a little more self awareness.**

In the past few years I've realized how helpful writing can be for processing my life, my feelings, my worries, and even my hobbies. Don't worry, I have a journaling app for all the gushy, feely stuff so that won't be here.

But self reflection is a game changer for personal growth, and growth leads to more joy, for me at least. So whether anyone reads this or not, the blog gives me an outlet to increase my own awareness, which makes me happier. I'm sure there's a scientific reason for it, but I just know from experience that it works.

**Community is a good thing.**

I have a lot of random interests and I'll probably write about most of them at one time or another. My hope is that like minded people will find me, connect with me, and community will grow out of it.

A genuine community where no one's trying to sell anyone anything and we just like talking about the same stuff. Something that, again, makes people happy.

**I like to create content.** 🤷‍♂️

When I was a kid I used to sit in my bedroom with a dual cassette boombox and pretend I was a radio DJ. I introduced each song to my stuffed animals in my best little radio voice.

![boombox](/images/boombox.jpg)

*The same boombox I had as a kid. Thank you, Google Images for unlocking this core memory.*

My Dad was a radio DJ before I was born, so maybe that inspired me. Or maybe I just loved music so much and back then the radio was where you heard it, so I wanted to be part of bringing that to the world.

![my dad on the radio](/images/my-dad-radio.jpg)

*My Dad. Live on the airwaves around 1978ish.*

Either way, as long as I can remember I've wanted to be a conduit for bringing media to people. Whether audio, video, photo, or text, I get excited about helping bring good content into the world. So don't be shocked if this site also includes video, audio, and/or musical content from time to time. It's in my blood.

## What can you expect?

Like all of us, my interests aren't limited to one thing. I've never been one to pigeonhole myself or stake my identity on one defining hobby. So this site will be an amalgamation of whatever occupies my brain on any given day.

**TV and movies**

This one has taken over more than I expected. I love psychological thrillers, crime dramas, and true crime, and I'm working my way through classics I never got around to. Expect reviews, reactions, and the occasional rant.

**Music**

Music has always been a major part of my life. I know, I know, everyone likes music. But my attachment to it borders on addiction. It can alter my state of mind. It's how I define sections of my life. Almost every impactful memory I have is tied to a song.

So there will be content about music. Album reviews, playlists I'm listening to, new artists I find, historical artists and songs. Wherever the music takes me, I'll go.

**Art and design**

Music is art, yes. But visual art and design is also a big part of my life. My day job is graphic and web design, so a lot of my brain power goes to creating, finding, and imitating good visual design.

These days that's mostly online through website design, but I've also started trying to learn how to draw. Pencil to paper, or Apple Pencil to iPad. So you'll likely see some of my artwork as I try to grow my skills, along with art and design I like and principles I'm learning.

![amateur sketch](/images/amateur-sketch.jpg)

*My goal is to learn how to draw this well or maybe even better!*

**Marketing**

Graphic designers are also marketers these days. My day job is aimed at digital marketing, as is my SEO business. With years of experience marketing products and services online, I have thoughts. Some days I may be discovering new principles or case studies, or just pointing out ads I think are effective. It's sure to creep into this site at some point.

**Family and parenting**

I can assure you, I will not get personal on this site. Mainly because I'm a fairly private person. However, I'm a husband and a dad, and those 2 roles are more important to me than any other. So I'll probably share stories, lessons learned, and general observations related to both.

**Other stuff**

Those are the main points, but the list of other random interests will keep creeping in too. Pro wrestling. Sports (Braves, Bengals, Man City). Technology. 80s and 90s nostalgia. Malls and dead malls.

So there we go. I'm doing this, and the place is open.

If you know me and consider me a friend, I'd love for you to follow along. If you're a stranger but some of my words resonate with you, I'd love for you to join me here.
